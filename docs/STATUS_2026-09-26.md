## Afternoon update: the resets, and the new pipeline
Danny's review of the paint-pinned 60 s set: the city looks good but it **morphs and resets constantly**, so it can't be used.
Cause (renders checked frame by frame): Wan `nokeep` makes every 41-frame window (1.7 s) from depth + one anchor,
with **no memory of the previous window**, so buildings, signs and trees are reinvented at each boundary (for example, C5
frames 618→624 swap a whole building and add a billboard). 3DREAL is faithful to its source, so it keeps the swaps.
**Fix: drop Wan. Unreal depth → LTX-2.3 + Lightricks IC-LoRA Union-Control (depth), one sampling pass over the
whole clip with overlapping context windows** (121 frames, overlap 48, guide strength 0.55, rich storefront prompt, per-camera
prompts in `prompts/ltx_union_1980s_C*_v2.txt`). The windows are fused at every denoising step, so the world is
continuous: buildings persist and recede, with no resets. It also looks more photoreal than Wan→3DREAL: lit storefronts, awnings,
full palms, pedestrians. About 4 min per 10 s, ~30 min per 60 s (vs 4.6 h Wan + 30 min 3DREAL).
`python scripts/ltx_3dreal.py renders/depth_C5_v15.mp4 --lora union --frames 1441 --context 121 --overlap 48 --guide_strength 0.55 --prompt_file prompts/ltx_union_1980s_C5_v2.txt`
Guide 1.0 = plain boxes (too literal); 0.5–0.7 = rich. Don't say "through the rear window" (it draws our own trunk).

## The pipeline that works
**Unreal (layout + real Dekogon cars) → Wan 2.2 Fun-VACE (anchor, no feed-forward) → LTX-2.3 + 3DREAL-light
(render-to-real re-render) → Magnific Topaz Starlight (upres).**

| Stage | What it contributes | Cost / time on the A6000 |
|---|---|---|
| Unreal 5.8 + Cesium, v15 world | Layout, traffic (weighted: vans/SUVs ~19%), passing lane, real car shapes, depth | ~8 min for 3 cams × 62 s |
| Wan 2.2 Fun-VACE, `PLATES_ANCHOR=1 PLATES_NOKEEP=1` | Invents the rich period content; every window from depth + one clean anchor frame, nothing fed forward, so it **cannot collapse** (seams remain) | ~4.6 h per 60 s |
| LTX-2.3 distilled fp8 + fal **3DREAL-light** IC-LoRA, context windows 121 f | Re-renders the whole clip coherently at 1280×704: **removes the Wan seams**, keeps layout and traffic, adds photoreal palms/storefronts/wires/cars | ~38 min per 60 s |
| Magnific Video Upscaler Topaz, `starlight_precise_2_5`, 2k | Faithful upres to 2560×1408, crisp detail, no content changes | ~46 min cloud per 60 s (credits) |

## What didn't work (this session) and why
- **Chaining Wan windows** (feeding generated frames forward): collapses between 15 and 30 s. The anchor delays, never prevents.
- **Colour / sky locks**: ghosts and see-through rectangles (depth's "sky" ≠ Wan's sky). Retired.
- **Crossfaded independent windows** (Wan no-feed-forward alone): no collapse but double exposures every 1.7 s.
- **Cosmos 3 Nano transfer**: rock-stable, excellent cars, but copies our crude geometry literally (stick palms, grey
  boxes); with a blurred-Wan hint it removes seams but palms stay sticks. 30 min per 5 s. Kept as an option.
- **Magnific upscalers alone**: sharpen, add no structural realism (by design; good for matching).
- **3DREAL on Unreal's own daylight render**: barely changes it (can't relight). It needs a look-dev'd source: Wan.
