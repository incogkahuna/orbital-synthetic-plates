In process work the camera faces the actors, so the LED wall mostly shows what is BEHIND and BESIDE the car. Priority: C5 rear (180°) > C3/C7 side profiles > C4/C6 rear three-quarters > C1 forward (least used). Danny corrected me when I proposed "forward 3 cameras" as the demo (2026-09-21).

**Why:** the plate has to match what the audience sees through the rear and side windows behind the actors.

**How to apply:** test and demo rear and side angles first. Rear plates need following traffic behind the camera car. Side views have fast lateral parallax, so expect the most flicker there. Related: [[orbital-plates-winning-look]]
